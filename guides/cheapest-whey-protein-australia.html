Why we rank by protein, not price

A big "70% off" sticker doesn't tell you much. Tubs come in different sizes, and some powders are only 65–70% protein while others are close to 90%. So we work out what 30g of actual protein costs from each tub, which is roughly one shake. That's the fairest way to compare a cheap concentrate with a pricier isolate.

A trial-size tub can be heavily discounted and still cost more per shake than a full-size tub at normal price. That's why a 76% off deal can rank below a 40% off one.

How to pay even less

  • Buy the bigger tub if you'll finish it. Price per 100g almost always drops as the size goes up.
  • Watch delivery. Most stores charge shipping under $150. Chemist Warehouse and other in-store chains let you pick up for free.
  • Check member prices. Some brands, like Musashi, show lower prices once you join their free program.
